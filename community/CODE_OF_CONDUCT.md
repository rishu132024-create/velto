# Velto Community Code of Conduct

The Velto community is intended to be welcoming and constructive.

## Respect

Treat other contributors with respect.

Do not harass, threaten, insult, or discriminate against other people.

## Collaboration

Discuss ideas based on their technical merits.

Give useful feedback.

Accept that contributors may have different approaches.

## Project Discussions

Keep discussions relevant to Velto.

Avoid spam and intentionally disruptive behavior.

## Security

Do not publicly expose passwords, API keys, bot tokens, private credentials, or other secrets.

Report security problems responsibly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when community rules are repeatedly violated.

The goal is to keep the Velto community useful and welcoming.
